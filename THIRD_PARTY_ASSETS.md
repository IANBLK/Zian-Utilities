- **The Dark Reaper Set** item and armor textures: MonGen's Cave.

For Bow Bundle Pack and The Dark Reaper Set, the project owner explicitly
confirmed written permission from both creators to include these assets in
the public CurseForge JAR and GitHub repository. This confirmation is recorded
as project-owner authorization; the original written permissions are held by
the owner and are not reproduced here. Keep all creator attributions.
